When the quantity to produce of an already confirmed Manufacturing Order
is changed (via the "Update Quantity" action), Odoo rescales the demand
of every open raw material move proportionally to the change --
including components that were added by hand, with no Bill of Materials
line behind them, and were never meant to vary with the batch size (for
example, a fixed kit of consumables used once per job regardless of how
many units are produced).

Because this rescale works off whatever is currently stored rather than
recomputing from the Bill of Materials, and always rounds up, a handful
of quantity changes back and forth is enough to turn clean, whole-unit
demands into fractional, physically meaningless ones (for example "1.34"
units of an item that cannot be split).

This module adds a "Fixed Quantity" flag on each raw material move of a
Manufacturing Order. A flagged move is skipped by that rescale and keeps
whatever quantity was entered for it, no matter how many times the
quantity to produce changes afterwards.

Raw material moves added without a Bill of Materials line of their own
are flagged "Fixed Quantity" automatically when created, since there is
no recipe tying their quantity to the batch size in the first place. The
flag can still be checked or unchecked by hand on any component.

Usage
=====

On a Manufacturing Order's Components tab, a "Fixed Qty" column is
available (enable it from the column selector if it isn't shown). Check
it on any component whose demand should stay exactly as entered
regardless of future changes to the order's quantity to produce.

Components added directly on the order, without picking them from the
Bill of Materials, are flagged this way automatically -- uncheck the box
on any of them if that particular one should actually scale with the
quantity produced.
